Jewish Cemeteries

  • Menachim Aveilim Informally called “Hebrew Rest” — Lafayette, Lee & College Avenues Land donated in 1869 by Gov. Alexandre Mouton; cemetery association chartered 1890
  • Gates of Prayer Cemetery New Iberia, West Main Street — cemetery land purchased in 1913
  • Hebrew Rest Cemetery Washington, Louisiana (St. Landry Parish) — a distinct cemetery from Lafayette’s informal “Hebrew Rest” name
  • Temple Emanuel / Gemiluth Chassodim Cemetery Opelousas, Bellevue Street
